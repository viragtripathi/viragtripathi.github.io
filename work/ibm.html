Make the platform work first

My role connected partner conversations, technical validation, and engineering delivery. A platform partnership needs working software underneath it. For CockroachDB and IBM, that meant validating distributed SQL on IBM architectures and addressing the compatibility issues that surfaced along the way.

What I led and built

I spearheaded the work from the first LinuxONE validation through official platform support and the IBM Power port. That engineering became part of the technical groundwork behind the OEM agreement IBM announced in October 2025.

I subsequently shipped the CockroachDB certification on IBM Power Systems and IBM Cloud, announced in May 2026. My contribution was the platform and technical enablement work within the broader IBM and Cockroach Labs partnership.

Where the interesting bugs live

Work across s390x and ppc64le exposed issues that a conventional deployment would not. I landed the ppc64le page-size fix in CockroachDB core, which shipped in v26.3.

Validating a cluster spanning IBM Z and x86 also surfaced a cross-architecture endianness problem in the vectorized engine. I filed it upstream with a workaround.

On the data side, I built an open-source pipeline connecting CockroachDB with IBM watsonx.data and Apache Iceberg, and presented that work at IBM's Tech Acceleration Workshop.

Engineering that reached the field

The work progressed from platform validation to official support, certification, and customer business through IBM.

I presented CockroachDB on IBM platforms at IBM Think and IBM TechXchange and wrote about LinuxONE for the IBM Community. IBM also recognized me as an IBM Champion.
